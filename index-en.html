Startup Act Tunisia (2019-2026)

Ongoing academic project — Professional Master’s in Competitive Intelligence, ESEN Manouba × ISCAE Manouba. Goal: measure the actual performance of Tunisia’s Startup Act labelling programme (Law 2018-20) by analysing all 88 sessions published on startup.gov.tn and presenting a reliable pre-label → label journey through an interactive dashboard.

Intelligence & Data in numbers

Real results from my quantitative study of Tunisia’s Startup Act programme — data extracted from the official PDFs published by startup.gov.tn (88 sessions, 2019–2026).

88Labelling sessions audited
3 574Applications processed
1 356Official labels
641Official pre-labels
1 343PDF-corrected labels
20/88Sessions with corrected discrepancies

Why were corrections needed? The official labelling PDFs did not match the “sessions” table on startup.gov.tn: 20 of 88 sessions contained incorrectly counted labels or pre-labels. The corrected figures were re-extracted and recalculated session by session, with 88/88 sessions audited and 20/88 sessions corrected.

⚠️ Note : the actual number of labelled startups is likely higher (estimated at more than 1,450), but these figures reflect exactly what is published on the official platform startup.gov.tn.
